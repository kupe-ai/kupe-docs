Kupe App `/api/call` webhooks and sockets, `create_call`, text/WhatsApp, Ultravox S2S. Narrative: [realtime](/migrate/realtime).

**34 unique Kupe App paths** in this table (56 method+path pairs). Inventory is the live FastAPI app, not `api_specification.yaml`.

<Note>
  Kupe App `api_specification.yaml` also lists `GET /api/call/test`. That path is **not mounted** in `the previous App backend` and has no Kupe Hub replacement.
</Note>
